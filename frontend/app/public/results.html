WHAT WE DID WELL
Strengths
  • Portfolio strategy worked. 5 temperature profiles (chalk/standard/smart_upset/cinderella/chaos) gave us coverage across upset scenarios. The cinderella brackets kept us alive through 8 first-round upsets.
  • Importance sampling was effective. We covered 0.00007% of the bracket space but were 2.4 million times more likely to have a perfect bracket than random selection.
  • Vegas odds integration. The 4-layer probability blend (market + stats + matchup + factors) with spread-adaptive weights produced well-calibrated probabilities.
  • Validity bitmap pruning. The architecture handled 206M brackets with <5 second prune times after optimization, using alive_outcome tables instead of scanning the full table.
  • 12 upsets predicted. Including TCU over Ohio State, VCU over UNC from 19 down, High Point over Wisconsin by 1 point, and 11-seed Texas's Cinderella run through Gonzaga.
  • Provable generation. SHA-256 hash committed to GitHub before tipoff. Immutable bracket table. Full audit trail.
WHAT TO REVISIT FOR 2027
Areas for Improvement
  • 1-seed upset coverage. Only 5.4% of alive brackets had Iowa over Florida. Our auto-advance rule (P=1.0 for 1/2 seeds in R64) was right, but later rounds need more upset mass for 1-seeds losing.
  • Calibration vs simulation disconnect. The matchup probabilities from the 4-layer blend (p_final) didn't fully flow into the simulation — the historical_patterns calibration step overrode some values. Need a single source of truth.
  • Coaching factor overhaul. Min-max normalization on coaching tourney appearances penalized young coaches at elite programs (Scheyer at Duke). Need program-aware coaching metric.
  • Mutation factor. The genetic algorithm / mutation approach was designed but never wired in. Adding a 6% mutation rate on coin-flip games would add diversity without changing the overall distribution.
  • R32+ probability model. The simulation uses base probability matrices for R32 onwards that diverge from the research module. Need unified probability pipeline from R64 through championship.
  • More aggressive upset allocation. Increase cinderella from 15% to 20%, reduce chalk from 30% to 25%. This tournament had 12 upsets through 47 games — our model expected ~13 total across 63.
  • Database performance. The 5-way JOIN across 206M rows was the persistent bottleneck. The alive_bracket_ids materialization fixed browsing, but pruning still took minutes early on. Consider partitioning by strategy or region.
  • Live odds updates. We used pre-tournament odds only. Incorporating live line movement during the tournament would improve later-round predictions.
